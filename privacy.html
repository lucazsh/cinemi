Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo solely to create your account. We also store content you create on the platform such as posts, reviews, and watchlist entries. If you choose to continue without an account, no personal data is collected.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the Cinemi service, including displaying your profile, saving your preferences, and enabling social features like posts and reviews. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Advertising (Google AdSense)

Cinemi uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other sites on the internet. Google's use of the DoubleClick cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting adssettings.google.com or aboutads.info. For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, visit policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Cookies

We use session cookies for authentication and local storage to remember your preferences. Google AdSense and other third-party vendors may set additional cookies for ad personalization and measurement. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

5. Third-party services

Cinemi uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In for authentication (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdSense for displaying ads (Google Ads Policy)
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) for movie data (TMDB Privacy Policy)

6. Data retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account exists. You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's privacy

Cinemi is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
